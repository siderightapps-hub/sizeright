How the calculation works

Risk management is the single most important skill in futures trading. This calculator uses a straightforward formula to tell you exactly how many contracts you can trade without exceeding your defined risk on any single trade.

Dollar risk = Account size × Risk %
Stop cost = Ticks × Tick value
Contracts = floor( Dollar risk ÷ Stop cost )

For example, with a $25,000 account risking 1% on NQ with a 10-tick stop:

Dollar risk = $25,000 × 1% = $250
Stop cost = 10 × $5 = $50 per contract
Contracts = floor( 250 ÷ 50 ) = 5 contracts

Always round down — never up. Taking on more contracts than your risk allows is how accounts blow up.
