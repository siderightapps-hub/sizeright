Privacy Policy

SizeRight ("the app") is operated by SideRight Apps. This policy explains what information the app handles, the third-party services it relies on, and your choices.

The Short Version

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, or any trading data. The figures you enter into the calculator are processed entirely on your device and are never sent to us.

We do not operate servers that store your personal data, and the app contains no analytics SDK. The app does, however, use third-party services for advertising (Google AdMob) and to process the optional Pro purchase (Apple and Google), which are described below.

Information Stored On Your Device

The app saves a small amount of data in your device's local storage. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • Your preferences, such as light/dark theme and whether you have completed the intro walkthrough.
  • Anonymous usage counters (how many days you have used the app), used only to decide when to show the "rate this app" prompt.
  • Your Pro entitlement status — whether you have unlocked the full instrument set.

This information is not linked to your identity and is not transmitted to us.

Advertising & Consent

The free version of the app displays banner ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as device and advertising identifiers, IP address, and data about how ads perform. Depending on your consent and region, ads may be personalised or non-personalised. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we present a consent message using Google's User Messaging Platform (a certified Consent Management Platform) before personalised ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time inside the app under Settings → Ad Privacy Options, and you can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device's system settings.

Pro Purchase

SizeRight offers a one-time "Pro" unlock. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple (on iOS, via the App Store) or Google (on Android, via Google Play Billing). We never see or store your payment-card details — we receive only confirmation of whether a purchase completed, which is used to unlock Pro on your device and to support "Restore Purchase". Apple's and Google's handling of your purchase is governed by their own privacy policies.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA), you may have rights over personal data. Because we do not hold personal data on our own servers, requests relating to advertising data should be directed to Google. You can manage advertising consent in-app under Settings → Ad Privacy Options and through your device settings. For anything else, contact us below.
