Help for Cheap Juice 1.0 · Updated 3 August 2026

Need help?

Cheap Juice shows public Agile electricity prices for your grid region. Try the quick answers below, or email Side Right Apps for help with a problem, feedback or a feature suggestion.

Quick answers

Prices are not updating

Check that your device is online, then pull down on the prices screen to refresh. The app shows when its data was last updated and whether the displayed prices are live or saved.

Tomorrow's prices are missing

Future Agile rates only appear after they have been published by the tariff provider. They generally arrive in the late afternoon or evening. Pull down to refresh again later.

The app shows the wrong region

Choose Change region on the prices screen. You can enter a postcode again or select your grid-supply region manually. Cheap Juice stores the selected region, not your postcode.

Why does the app say “saved prices”?

Cheap Juice keeps the latest valid prices on your device so they remain visible without a connection. Reconnect to the internet and refresh to request the newest available prices.

How do I install an available app update?

Cheap Juice checks for compatible updates when it opens and when you refresh. If an update has downloaded, the app reloads to apply it.

Contact support

Email us with a description of what happened. The app's About section can create a beta diagnostics summary for you to share; it does not include your postcode.

Helpful details include:

  • Your device model and operating-system version.
  • The Cheap Juice app and build version.
  • Your selected pricing region.
  • Any error message shown in the app.

Privacy and data

Cheap Juice has no user account and does not require energy-account credentials, payment details or smart-meter readings. Read the full Cheap Juice Privacy Policy for details about postcode lookup, locally saved settings and Expo app updates.

Independent app

Cheap Juice uses publicly available Octopus Energy tariff information. It is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Octopus Energy Ltd.
