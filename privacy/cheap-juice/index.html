Cheap Juice

Privacy Policy

Effective 2 August 2026

The short version

Cheap Juice has no account and does not require your Octopus Energy credentials. Your selected region, rate preferences and a recent copy of public tariff prices stay on your device. If you enter a postcode, it is sent directly to Octopus Energy to identify your electricity-supply region and is not saved by the app. Side Right Apps does not sell your data.

Who provides Cheap Juice

Cheap Juice is provided by Side Right Apps (“we”, “us” or “our”). This policy explains how the Cheap Juice mobile app handles information.

Postcode and electricity region

Postcode lookup is optional. If you use it, the UK postcode you enter is sent securely over HTTPS directly to Octopus Energy's public grid-supply-point service solely to identify your electricity-supply region. Cheap Juice does not send the postcode to a Side Right Apps server and does not save it on your device after the lookup.

The resulting grid region is saved on your device. You can avoid providing a postcode by choosing your region manually.

Octopus Energy may receive ordinary network information, such as your IP address and request time, when its API is contacted. Its handling of information is governed by the Octopus Energy Privacy Policy.

Information stored on your device

Cheap Juice stores the following information locally:

  • Your selected electricity-supply region.
  • Your chosen rate threshold and cheapest-window duration.
  • Recently downloaded tariff identifiers, electricity rates and fetch times for offline use.

Side Right Apps does not receive this locally stored information on a server.

Public electricity-price data

Cheap Juice requests public Agile tariff and half-hourly unit-rate information directly from the Octopus Energy API. It does not ask for an Octopus account number, API key, smart-meter readings, payment information or account-specific consumption data.

Cheap Juice is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Octopus Energy Ltd.

App updates

Cheap Juice uses Expo's EAS Update service to check for and download compatible app updates when the app opens and when you refresh it. Expo may process technical information needed to provide this service, including your operating system, app project information, IP address, errors or performance information, and a random update token.

See the Expo Privacy Policy and Expo's privacy explanation.

Diagnostics you choose to share

The About section can create beta diagnostic text containing the app and build version, device model, operating-system version, selected region, tariff identifiers, fetch time, rate-slot count, saved or live status and the most recent error.

This information is created locally and is not sent automatically. The app opens your device's share sheet, and you decide whether to share it and with whom. Diagnostics never include your postcode. Once shared, the recipient service's privacy policy applies.

What Cheap Juice does not do

The current version:

  • Does not create user accounts.
  • Does not include advertising or advertising tracking.
  • Does not include behavioural analytics.
  • Does not sell personal information.
  • Does not provide purchases or subscriptions.
  • Does not collect payment or energy-account credentials.

Retention and deletion

Local settings and cached prices remain on your device until they are replaced, you clear the app's storage, or you uninstall the app. Clearing Cheap Juice's storage in device settings or uninstalling the app deletes the information held locally by the app.

Cheap Juice has no user-account database and therefore holds no account data to delete remotely. Information independently processed by Octopus Energy or Expo is subject to their respective policies.

Children

Cheap Juice is a household electricity-price utility intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

You can:

  • Choose your region manually instead of entering a postcode.
  • Decline to share diagnostics.
  • Clear locally stored app data through device settings or by uninstalling.
  • Contact us about applicable privacy rights concerning information sent directly to us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. We will update the effective date on this page and, where appropriate, provide notice in the app.
